wrong in two ways here:

The stalled solver had been hiding this. These are the advisor's candidates 2 (neighbours in the foreign density) and
4 (birth from the seed's own spread), now exposed. Bed w also stays over-confident (rms 0.14 m, 50 % of rows > 3σ).
