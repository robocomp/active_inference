## 4. Every FAIL, with its cause

1. **Yaw σ below the model's lower bound** (nightstand and bed, 2 and 3 checks): a **registered omission**.
   - The per-DOF model has no once-entered room-axis or wall terms, so its yaw band (lo ≈ 0.013 rad) cannot reach the engine's σ_yaw of 0.003.
   - The failure is in the test model, not evidence of an engine fault. The yaw errors themselves are −2.5σ (nightstand) and −2.8σ (bed): the engine's yaw is also overconfident.
2. **Drop at B outside the band.**
   - Final engine: the bed's cy drop is 0.931, **above** the band 0.679…0.851. B was **less** informative than the per-DOF model predicts.
   - Earlier runs: B was **more** informative than predicted (2cb59a19 nightstand cx 0.740 < 0.829; bed h 0.589 < 0.697).
   - Cause: **unexplained.** The per-DOF model leaves out cross-DOF coupling, the wall terms and the driving views' fresh share, which the predictions registered as uncovered. Which of these moves the drop, and in which direction per run, was not diagnosed.
3. **Nightstand: net A2 0.539 > net B 0.107** (the registered inequality fails; both nets are inside their own bands).
   - Cause: **the inequality was a wrong prediction.** The registered derivation (1 − ρ)² ≥ 0 assumes two equal views with one shared ρ.
   - Under the logged ρ the registered model itself predicts the opposite order:
     - net B in 0.000…0.276 (band −0.572…0.848 without the tolerance);
     - net A2 in 0.965…1.698 (0.393…2.270 without the tolerance).
   - The logged ρ_cam into B was 0.045; into A2 it was 6e-8, after a longer drive with more distinct views.
   - The check contradicts the band check made from the same model, and the band check is the one derived from the logged inputs.
   - Not diagnosed further from the dumps.
4. **Toilet: no node matched** (d² 126.9; error −24 cm along the depth, −15.7σ) — **model choice**.
   - One box is fitted to a bowl + tank shape. The camera sees two front layers 0.54 m apart, and the box front lands between them.
   - The back is held at the wall by wall-hitting beam rays labelled toilet, together with the stand-off prior (0.03 ± 0.02 m; this world puts it at 0.18 m).
   - σ sits at the manifest's shape floors (h 0.0234, w 0.043, H 0), which do not cover the step or the unseen lid (`task-P1-toilet-bias-diagnosis.md`).
   - **Goes to K2 as a bowl + tank box-part model.** No interim widening of the spreads (owner ruling: that would be a fitted bound).
5. **Overconfidence: NEES/DOF 2.31 (nightstand), 3.49 (bed), 40.5 (toilet)**, against about 1.
   - Nightstand and bed: **unexplained** beyond the registered omissions, which leave the single-object σ at the shape-latent floors with no coupling terms. The yaw (−2.5σ / −2.8σ) and the bed's cy and h (+2.2σ / +2.0σ) carry most of it.
   - Toilet: cause 4.
6. **The false move after a viewpoint gap** (a recorded limitation, not in the f3f60a4f headline runs).
   - In `p1r7_bed` upd 1572, a LiDAR-only frame after a 48-cycle gap got P(moved) 0.9987 on a static bed.
   - The θ-only stop rule leaves the LiDAR's measured state 0.11 rad from its mode, and the move evidence is read there.
   - That visit's net A2 was −2.44 nats. Every fix tried trades it for GN cost (2.3–6.7×), detectability, or a new false move (`task-F1r8-report.md`).
   - **The headline runs had no false move:** 0 DEGENERATE, and their nets are in band.

## 5. Engine changes P1 caused (F1 rounds 7 and 8)

### Round 7 A2 (3a5b418f): the move evidence in information form on the joint state

- **Defect.** One update drove log Q to +134: a 2.8e-45 denormal in the innovation covariance, then a non-finite P and a DEGENERATE re-seed (`p1_bed`, cycle 153).
- **Fix.** The Bayes factor is computed through 𝟙 + ΛP, which is never singular.

### Round 7 A1 (65ece686): the move evidence read at the stayed posterior mode, not at the prediction

- **Defect.** False moves on moving LiDAR-only frames: `p1_bed` cycles 1708 and 1709, P 1.0 and 0.9992. They produced visit net A2 −5.83 nats.
- **Replay on `p1_bed`:**
  - P(moved) > 1e-3 went from 3 updates to 0;
  - DEGENERATE from 1 to 0;
  - visit net A2 from −5.83 to +0.42 (in band).
- **Cost.** A 0.3 m LiDAR-only move is still not detected, as before. A 0.5 m one is detected after 30 updates, against 19.

### Round 7 B (a34f49db, bcece30f): depth-ordered beam labels — **reverted in round 8 (f3f60a4f)**

- B un-labelled the true bed top behind a narrow birth box (P(behind) 0.90–0.98, m ≈ 0.09).
- The box then held itself narrow: `p1r7_bed` was not matched (d² 14.1).
