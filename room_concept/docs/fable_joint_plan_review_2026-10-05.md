# Review: joint sensor-mount + odometry calibration plan (2026-10-05) — Fable, read-only

## Summary in plain words

1. The lever-arm formula in Task 1 describes an episode as one big turn measured once at its end. The real estimator adds up many small corrections, each measured in the robot's heading at that moment (96 % of corrections are one cycle apart), so the right lever regressor is the sum of those small pieces. With today's episode sizes (0.8 rad) the plan's formula is 10 % low on one component and invents a second column that is 40 % of the real one; the selftest generates data with the same wrong formula, so it would pass anyway. Fix: accumulate the two lever covariates per correction and regress on them.
2. Three sign conventions do not agree with each other: the helios yaw ↔ eps_yaw sign (I derive −1, the plan defaults to +1), the direction of the point correction in Task 5 (the plan applies the inverse of what cancels a mount error, so a correct estimate would double the planted error in arm B), and what the injection numbers mean (the plan's injection plants the negative of what the report then grades against, so a correct estimator fails check 1 and a sign-flipped one passes). Define everything through one "mount pose" M = (lever, ψ_h): points are undone by M⁻¹, corrected by M, injected by M_inj⁻¹.
3. The camera block is pose-free, so it does not double count the motion block's corrections; the only shared quantity is the helios mount. But the lever arm (not just the yaw) enters every camera pair as a corner shift the camera's four parameters cannot express, and the plan's "prior stays on the relative mount" ties the camera yaw to the LiDAR yaw with correlation 0.94 before any data — put the prior on the body-frame yaw and change the reduction test accordingly.
4. The lever is observable from episodes, and net rotation is enough once the regressor is accumulated per correction. But with today's episode weights (σ_pos ≈ 79 mm because the rotation-model term already charges turning episodes for exactly the effect the lever now models) the current evidence file pins a lever to about ±16 mm: a 3 cm injection is a 2σ detection. Drop the rotation-model term when the lever acts, grade on "informed" and on detection, not on "within 2σ of the truth" (which the prior alone passes for the 0.5° yaw).
5. Switching the yaw from the odometry side to the LiDAR side must NOT remove it from `p_applied` (the plan clears its apply bit): that reproduces the 50 % cap the plan itself warns about. Record the applied yaw regardless of which side applies it; only the odometry-side consumer returns 0. Tonight's GT error has no lever-arm signature (≤ 1 cm, inconsistent across runs, explains 1 mm of 42 mm rms), so the slow pose-field bias from my window memo is not a helios lever and arm C should come out near zero.

## Q1. The math

### 1a. Lever Jacobian — model mismatch with how episodes are formed (Task 1, Steps 1 and 3)

Per correction k, with Δθ_k the rotation since the previous correction and Δ the sensor position in body coordinates (published = true + R(θ)Δ): r_k = R(θ_k)ᵀ[R(θ_k)Δ − R(θ_{k−1})Δ] = (I − R(Δθ_k)ᵀ)Δ. The episode residual is the sum over its corrections, so the lever term is

    Σ_k (I − R(Δθ_k)ᵀ) Δ = [ Σ(1−cos Δθ_k)   −Σ sin Δθ_k ; Σ sin Δθ_k   Σ(1−cos Δθ_k) ] Δ     (rows: X lateral, Y forward)

and NOT (I − R(Δθ_net)ᵀ)Δ. The two agree only when the episode contains a single correction at its end. Live, corrections come one cycle apart (782 of 812 scored corrections in `heading_2026-10-05_19-58-02.csv` have `sur_open = 1`), so Δθ_k ≈ ω·0.05 s and Σ sin Δθ_k ≈ Δθ_net, Σ(1−cos Δθ_k) ≈ ½ΣΔθ_k² ≈ 0. At the 0.8 rad episode close: plan's sin(0.8) = 0.72 vs the real ≈ 0.80 (−10 %), and the plan's (1−cos 0.8) = 0.30 vs ≈ 0.0 — a spurious column 40 % of the real one that cross-couples lever_x and lever_y. At the file's 1.73 rad maximum: 0.99 vs 1.73 (−43 %). Intermediate headings do NOT matter (the sum telescopes to first order), so net rotation is the right covariate — but with the LINEAR map, and exactly with the per-correction sums.

**Task 1, Step 3: change** the rows to use two accumulated covariates instead of `cos/sin(e.d_theta)`:
- `Episode` gains `float lever_s = 0, lever_c = 0;` (Σ sin Δθ_k, Σ (1−cos Δθ_k)); written as two more fields at the END of the `E` row.
- `MotionCalibrator::observe()`: keep `th_since_corr_ += d_theta` every cycle; on a `corrected` cycle `acc_lever_s_ += sin(th_since_corr_); acc_lever_c_ += 1 − cos(th_since_corr_); th_since_corr_ = 0;` (reset with the episode).
- rows: `j_along[P_LEVER_X] = e.lever_s; j_along[P_LEVER_Y] = e.lever_c; j_cross[P_LEVER_X] = e.lever_c; j_cross[P_LEVER_Y] = −e.lever_s;` (signs as in the plan — they are right for r = est − pred in the current-heading frame).
- loader: legacy rows get `lever_s = d_theta, lever_c = 0` — the first-order value, an APPROXIMATION, not the "exact" padding the plan claims for `p_applied` (that claim is right for p_applied only).

**Task 1, Step 1: change** `lever_rows` to build each episode as a sum of 10-60 sub-corrections with small Δθ_k (the live regime) and add one episode class with a single end correction at |Δθ| up to 1.5 rad (the rare regime); generate `r` from the per-correction model and fill `lever_s/lever_c` the same way the live code does. Case 7A then tests the estimator against the data it will actually see.

### 1b. The eps_yaw ↔ ψ_h sign (Task 3, `kEpsPerLidarYaw`)

A sensor yawed by +ψ_h (CCW) reads bearings −ψ_h, so the ingested points are p_nom = R(−ψ_h)(p_b − Δ) and the localiser, aligning R(θ_est)p_nom + t to the walls, reports θ_est = θ_true + ψ_h (position + R(θ)Δ). The odometry step is integrated at θ_est (+ eps, room_concept.cpp:8072): R(θ_true + ψ_h + eps)·(0, d). It is cancelled — i.e. the closed loop converges — iff eps = −ψ_h. This does not depend on the row's sign convention, only on where the value is applied. So **kEpsPerLidarYaw = −1**, and A(yaw, eps) = kCam/kEps = +1 (y_rel = ψ_c + eps). Keep Task 6 check 2 as the arbiter, but set the default to −1 and record this derivation next to the constant. The camera constant: the pair's yaw column is a rotation of the PREDICTED corners about camera-up by +x, x = −p; the image shows R(−ψ_c)p_b, the prediction R(−ψ_h)p_b ⇒ x = ψ_h − ψ_c ⇒ p = ψ_c − ψ_h ⇒ **kCamPerLidarYaw = −1 as the plan has**, under the camera convention "p = physical CCW yaw of the camera", which image_edge_source.cpp:268-272 says was verified symbolically.

### 1c. The point correction and the injection (Task 5, Step 3; Task 6, Step 1; `joint_calib_report.py`)

Define the mount pose M = Planar{lever_x, lever_y, ψ_h}. Then p_nom = M⁻¹(p_b) (from 1b), so:
- **correction:** p_b = M(p_nom) ⇒ `T_corr = Planar{+lever_x, +lever_y, +eps/kEpsPerLidarYaw}` — the plan's `{−lever_x, −lever_y, −eps/kEps}` has both signs inverted relative to its own Task 1 definition; with the plan's `kEps = +1` that is exactly `inverse(M)` to first order, and arm B would DOUBLE the planted error.
- **injection:** to plant a mount error M_inj, transform the nominal points by `T_inject = inverse(Planar{InjectX, InjectY, InjectYaw})`, and document InjectX/Y/Yaw as "the sensor's displacement and CCW yaw from its nominal mount, body frame". Then `compose(T_corr, T_inject) = M_est ∘ M_inj⁻¹ = identity` when the estimate is right, and the report compares `lever_x` to `+InjectX`, `lever_y` to `+InjectY`, `eps/kEps` to `+InjectYaw` SIGNED (the report's `abs(abs(eps) − abs(yaw))` throws away the very sign check 2 needs; print the signed value and the implied kEps instead).
- As written, `T_inject = {x, y, yaw}` applied directly plants M_inj ≈ {(−x, −y), −yaw}, so `--inject 0.03 -0.02 0.5` grades a correct estimator as wrong (Q5, first item).
- Use `inverse()` rather than `{−x, −y, −yaw}` wherever an inverse is meant (the difference is 0.3 mm here, but the selftest already pins `inverse`).

## Q2. Double counting and the prior

- **No pose double count.** The pairs never see the localiser pose (mount_lidar_pair.h:20-35), so camera evidence and motion episodes are conditionally independent given T_bh. The solve-path `pos_var` fed to episodes is the post-floor published covariance (4166 before 4401) — a weight that depends on the correction it weighs; the early-exit path uses the pre-floor value (5183 before 5207). Make both pre-floor (the memo's seed fix is the same defect one layer up); this is where the main session's innovation work will land (plan follow-on #3) — keep `pos_var` semantically "variance of r" so Task 1's tests do not bake in today's source.
- **A coupling the plan omits: the lever enters the camera pairs.** p_robot = R(−ψ_h)(p_b − Δ): a 3 cm lever displaces every LiDAR corner by ≈ 3 cm in the robot plane, which at 3 m range is 0.57° of bearing — the same order as the planted yaw. It is not constant per vertex (it depends on range and bearing), so it loads partly on the camera yaw and partly on the per-vertex nuisance. It cannot be added post hoc to the marginal information; it needs lever columns in `PairObs::J` (the "LiDAR leg" note at mount_lidar_pair.h:232 already contemplates the geometry). **Task 6, Step 1: change** check 2 to a YAW-ONLY injection (InjectX = InjectY = 0) for the sign arbitration; grade the lever in a separate leg or accept that `<cam>.yaw` will move by more than ψ_h when the lever is planted.
- **"Prior stays on the relative mount" is the wrong choice.** A prior N(0, σ_c²) on ψ_c − ψ_h together with N(0, σ_h²) on ψ_h implies ψ_c ~ N(0, σ_c² + σ_h²) correlated with ψ_h at ρ = σ_h/√(σ_c² + σ_h²) = 0.94 for σ_h = 0.57° (MotionCalibYawP0) and σ_c = 0.2° (mount yaw sigma) — before any data the joint says the camera is yawed almost exactly as the LiDAR. Physically the two mounts are independent. **Task 3, Step 3: change** to `H += Aᵀ H_data A + I_cᵀ H_prior I_c; b += Aᵀ b_data + I_cᵀ b_prior` with I_c the identity on the camera's own columns (no eps coupling in the prior term), which needs **Task 2** to return `b_data` and `b_prior` separately (it already computes them separately: `bm` and the prior pull). The anchor stays `applied` (the LiDAR-applied yaw is 0 until Task 5; after that the anchor shifts by it). **Task 3, reduction test: change** the expectation: with eps at its prior only and a precise y_rel, the joint gives ψ_c = y_rel·σ_c²/(σ_c² + σ_h²) and eps/kEps = −y_rel·σ_h²/(σ_c² + σ_h²) — the relative measurement is SHARED between the mounts in proportion to their prior variances; "joint == chained" holds only in the limit σ_h → 0 (helios yaw known). Test both limits.
- **After Task 5 applies the yaw to the points, new pairs measure ψ_c − (ψ_h − ψ_applied)** while the motion block's eps (through p_applied) stays a TOTAL. Mixed evidence cannot be re-coupled from one marginal. **Task 5, Step 3: add** — when the applied LiDAR yaw changes by d, re-anchor every camera Accum with the existing `r → r − J d` path (mount_lidar_pair.h:476-517), d on the yaw column = −d_ψ in the camera's sign, so the camera evidence stays expressed against the ORIGINAL LiDAR frame and A with the total eps remains valid.

## Q3. Observability of the lever from episodes, and the existing logs

- Net rotation is enough (1a): the per-correction sum telescopes to Δθ_net·JΔ at first order; r_X ≈ −Δθ·Δ_Y, r_Y ≈ +Δθ·Δ_X, so lever_X comes from the along row and lever_Y from the cross row, both linear in net rotation. The (1−cos) column only appears when a single correction spans a large turn.
- Excitation today (etc/motion_calib_state.csv, 109 episodes): Σ dθ²/pos_var = 3724 m⁻² ⇒ σ_lever ≈ 16 mm from this file alone; the eps side Σ d_fwd²/pos_var = 6926 ⇒ σ_eps ≈ 0.69°, i.e. NOT informed against the 0.57° prior. A weighted regression of r_lat and r_fwd on dθ gives −4.5 ± 17 mm/rad and +4.2 ± 17 mm/rad — no lever signal in the uninjected sim, consistent with an exact mount. (The fusion/switch files from 10-01 are worse: 6 % of episodes above 0.5 rad.)
- The weight is the problem, not the excitation: σ_pos ≈ 79 mm per episode because `rot_model_sigma = 0.030 m/rad` (config:1032) and `fit_model_gain·max|SDF|` are added in quadrature. The rotation term exists to absorb the lever effect the plan now models; keeping it while the lever is estimated charges turning episodes twice and caps σ_lever near 15 mm however long the run. **Task 5, Step 3: add** — when `LidarMountApply` is on (lever acting), set the rotation-model contribution to 0 (or to the lever's posterior σ·|dθ|, which is the honest residual); **Task 1** keeps it, as planned, so the arm A estimate is conservative.
- Tonight's GT: a heading-rotating fit e = R(θ)L + const over 911° of heading gives L = (−8.6, +5.7) mm whole-run and (−1.5, +9.6) mm on 447 pivot rows (12-07-20: (−2.2, +6.2) / (−10.5, +3.5)); it removes 1 mm of a 42 mm rms. So the slow pose-field bias in my window memo is NOT a helios lever arm, and arm C should read |L| ≲ 1 cm. No conflict with the memo's model; the two can be told apart precisely because the lever rotates with heading and the field does not.

## Q4. Task 5 — frames and the odometry/LiDAR-side rule

- Applying a planar transform in `pump()` after the device→body poll (lidar_ingestor.cpp:129) is the right place: the height band filter is z-only and unaffected; the corner detector, wall-SLAM, the SDF and the camera pairs all consume the corrected points, so the map and the pairs are built in ONE frame. bpearl is a separate reader and stays uncorrected (fine: nothing fuses the two planar).
- Turning `LidarMountApply` on mid-run moves the published pose by R(θ)Δ and ψ_h in one step (the map anchors the room frame). Expected; note it in the config comment so the jump is not read as a relocalisation.
- **The apply-mask rule is wrong as written.** Clearing the eps bit makes `acting(P_EPS_YAW)` false ⇒ `p_applied[P_EPS_YAW] = 0` (motion_calibration.h:520) while the yaw IS acting on the points ⇒ the undo `r + J·p_applied` no longer undoes it ⇒ the solve returns the RESIDUAL yaw, T_corr applies it as a total, and the loop converges to half the error — Review Focus #2 exactly. **Task 5, Step 3: change** to: `acting(P_EPS_YAW)` unchanged (p_applied records the value that acted, whichever side applied it); add `Config::lidar_side_yaw` and make `yaw_offset()` (motion_calibration.h:249, the odometry-side consumer at room_concept.cpp:8072) return 0 when it is set. Same for the lever: `apply_lever` only gates `acting()`; there is no odometry-side consumer.
- The per-cycle residual's body axes use the published heading (7295-7301); before the LiDAR-side correction that heading carries ψ_h, after it does not — second order for a 0.5° yaw, ignore.
